# Financial, Deductible & Quantum Guidelines for ULA Adjusters

## 1. Compound & Percentage Deductibles
Policies frequently express deductibles with compound formulas, for example:
- *"10% of claim value, minimum EUR 750.00"*
- *"5% of adjusted loss, min $1,000 max $10,000"*
- *"1% of sum insured"*

### Calculation Rules:
1. **Never conflate the percentage and minimum amount** into a single concatenated number (e.g. "10% min 750" must NOT become "10,750").
2. Calculate the percentage against the adjusted loss:
   $$\text{Percentage Amount} = \text{Adjusted Loss} \times \frac{\text{Percent}}{100}$$
3. Apply minimum/maximum caps:
   - If $\text{Percentage Amount} < \text{Minimum}$, apply the **Minimum**.
   - If $\text{Percentage Amount} > \text{Maximum}$ (when a max is specified), apply the **Maximum**.
   - Otherwise, apply the calculated percentage amount.
4. If the claim quantum is provisional or unconfirmed, state the deductible as:
   `"10% of claim value, minimum [Currency] [Amount] (provisional application: [Calculated Amount])"`.

## 2. Invoices: Numeric vs Amount in Words Discrepancies
- Invoices may contain typographical errors where the numeric total (e.g. `EUR 87,982.51`) does not match the amount written in letters.
- Do NOT blindly declare underinsurance based on a minor mathematical subtraction if a clerical discrepancy exists between numbers and words.
- Clearly state: *"The invoice records a numerical total of [X] but a conflicting amount in words, while documented insured value is [Y]. The invoice amount and applicable valuation basis require professional reconciliation."*

## 3. Replacement Quotations vs Gross Claim
- A replacement quotation (e.g. from a stonemason, contractor, or supplier) evidences proposed repair/replacement costs.
- It does not automatically prove that:
  - All items were accepted as covered under the policy;
  - VAT is irrecoverable by the Assured (if the Assured is VAT-registered, VAT is deducted from indemnity);
  - Salvage value of damaged goods has been credited.
- Clearly present provisional adjustments as:
  $$\text{Replacement Quotation Total} - \text{Applicable Deductible} - \text{Salvage/Uncovered} = \text{Provisional Amount after Deductible}$$

## 4. Valuation Uplift
- Standard marine cargo policies often insure on a basis of **Invoice Value + 10% (CIF + 10%)**.
- If evidenced, apply the 10% uplift to the base cargo loss before deducting deductible and salvage.

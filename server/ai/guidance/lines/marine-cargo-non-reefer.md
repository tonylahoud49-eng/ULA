# Marine Cargo (Non-Reefer) Specialist Guidance

## 1. Scope & Common Commodities
- General containerized cargo, manufactured goods, machinery, stone/marble/granite slabs, glass, ceramics, textiles, and dry commodities.

## 2. Key Evidentiary & Policy Checks
- **Transport Documents**:
  - Compare Master Bill of Lading (ocean carrier) with House Bill of Lading (freight forwarder).
  - Explicitly verify:
    - **Vessel / Voyage**: Flag discrepancies (e.g. Master B/L naming *CMA CGM NANSHA / 0NVMHN1MA* while House B/L names *CMA CGM TOKYO*).
    - **Parties**: Differentiate Assured, House Shipper (e.g. supplier), House Consignee (buyer), and Master Consignee (clearing forwarder).
    - **Clauses / Notations**: Check for *Clean on Board*, *Free In / Free Out (FIO)*, *Shipper's Load Stow and Count (SLAC)*, *FCL/FCL*.
- **Warranties & Conditions**:
  - **Professional Packing Warranty**: Inspect photo evidence of crates, internal bracing, strapping, void-fill, and timber framing. If crates collapsed internally, note: *"Observed crate-frame collapse and breakage are compatible with loss of restraint, cargo movement or handling impact, subject to investigation of packing adequacy and void filling."*
  - **Preliminary Survey Warranty**: Check if policy mandates a preliminary inspection at customs warehouse upon first signs of damage. If delivered directly to final site without customs survey, flag as a compliance item for professional review.
  - **Under-Deck Carriage**: Containerized cargo carried on cellular container vessels satisfies under-deck warranty via standard container vessel clauses.

## 3. Adjustment & Loss Quantification
- Reconcile replacement quotes, supplier invoices, packing/freight costs, and salvage value.
- Reconcile invoice numerical values against written amounts.
- Apply compound deductibles (e.g. 10% of claim, minimum EUR 750).

# Air Cargo (NET) Specialist Guidance

## 1. Scope & Common Commodities
- High-value electronics, pharmaceuticals, luxury goods, express shipments, perishables transported under Air Waybill (AWB).

## 2. Key Evidentiary & Legal Checks
- **Air Waybill (AWB) Particulars**:
  - Differentiate Master AWB (airline) vs House AWB (forwarder).
  - Record Gross Weight vs Volumetric / Chargeable Weight.
  - Flight numbers, transshipment hubs, and arrival timestamps.
- **Notice of Claim & Airline Liability**:
  - Check compliance with Montreal Convention 1999 Article 31 notice deadlines (14 days for cargo damage, 21 days for delay).
  - Airline statutory liability limitation: 22 SDR per kilogram (Montreal Convention).
- **Physical Loss & Survey**:
  - Property Irregularity Report (PIR), airport warehouse receipt conditions, packaging adequacy for air transit vibrations/pressurization.

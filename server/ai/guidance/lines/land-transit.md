# Land Transit & Road Haulage Specialist Guidance

## 1. Scope & Conveyance
- Road transport, domestic trucking, international haulage (CMR), rail freight.

## 2. Key Evidentiary & Carriage Checks
- **CMR / Consignment Note**:
  - Consignor, carrier, consignee, truck registration, trailer number, driver identity.
  - CMR Article 23 carrier liability limits: 8.33 SDR per gross kilogram.
  - Driver accident reports, police traffic collision reports, cargo shifting during emergency braking, hijack / theft from unsecured parking.

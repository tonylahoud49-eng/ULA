# Fidelity Guarantee & Commercial Crime Specialist Guidance

## 1. Scope & Incident Types
- Employee theft, embezzlement, fraudulent ledger entries, inventory misappropriation, unauthorized funds transfers.

## 2. Key Evidentiary & Accounting Checks
- **Accounting & Audit Trails**:
  - Reconcile general ledger balances against bank statements, invoices, and physical counts.
  - Establish exact dates/periods of fraudulent acts and verify discovery period compliance under the policy.
  - Employee status, employment contract, role description, and internal control/dual-authorization safeguards.
- **Police & Investigation Records**:
  - Official police report, suspect confessions/interviews, forensic accountant reports, recoveries/restitutions made to date.

# Commercial & Residential Property Specialist Guidance

## 1. Scope & Perils
- Commercial buildings, industrial warehouses, residential real estate, machinery breakdown, storm, water leak, fire, flood, collapse.

## 2. Key Evidentiary & Scope Checks
- **Incident & Structural Causation**:
  - Independent structural engineer / fire investigator findings.
  - Distinguish covered peril (e.g. storm damage, burst pipe) from excluded wear-and-tear, gradual seepage, defective design, or lack of maintenance.
- **Quantum & Betterment**:
  - Itemized contractor repair tenders and material bills of quantities.
  - Apply depreciation / betterment adjustments where old structures/machinery are replaced with new materials.
  - Deduct salvage, apply policy sub-limits, and check co-insurance / average condition clauses for underinsurance.

# Bulk Vessel Cargo Specialist Guidance

## 1. Scope & Common Commodities
- Bulk grains, wheat, corn, soya, fertilizers, minerals, coal, cement, crude/refined oils, bulk chemicals.

## 2. Key Evidentiary & Quantity Checks
- **Draft Surveys & Weight Determination**:
  - Initial vs Final draft surveys by independent marine surveyors.
  - Shore scale weight vs Vessel draft survey displacement figures.
  - Standard ocean transit trade allowance (e.g. 0.5% customary transit loss for dry bulk).
- **Cargo Quality & Condition**:
  - FOSFA / GAFTA / BIMCO certificates of quality, moisture content, heat damage, mold, water ingress through hatch covers, ullage soundings, bunker contamination.

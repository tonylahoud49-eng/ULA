# Marine Cargo (Reefer / Perishables) Specialist Guidance

## 1. Scope & Common Commodities
- Temperature-controlled perishables, frozen meats/poultry, fresh produce (fruits, vegetables), dairy, pharmaceuticals, and GFS supplies.

## 2. Key Evidentiary & Technical Checks
- **Reefer Downloads & Temperature Data**:
  - Differentiate between **Supply Air**, **Return Air**, **Ambient Temp**, and **Pulp Temperature** taken at unstuffing.
  - Review reefer data logger logs, partlow charts, and power-off periods during transshipment or port dwell.
  - Distinguish between:
    - Active mechanical breakdown of reefer machinery (often covered under frozen meat / perishable clauses if exceeding specified hours, e.g., 24 consecutive hours);
    - Pre-cooling / pre-shipment thermal abuse (cargo loaded warm);
    - Inherent vice / natural ripening / pathological decay.
- **Shortage vs Intact Seals**:
  - Verify seal numbers at origin, discharge port, and receiver's warehouse.
  - Reconcile carton/case counts against packing lists and bills of lading.

## 3. Adjustment & Loss Quantification
- Check sound vs damaged market values, salvage sales proceeds, dumping/destruction certificates, and sorting/re-packing costs.

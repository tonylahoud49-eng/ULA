# Marine Hull & Yacht Specialist Guidance

## 1. Scope & Vessel Types
- Pleasure craft, luxury motor yachts, sailing yachts, catamarans, tenders, and commercial workboats.

## 2. Key Evidentiary & Hull Checks
- **Casualty & Survey Particulars**:
  - Grounding, collision, fire, sinking, machinery breakdown, mast/rigging failure, storm damage.
  - Marine surveyor inspection report, haul-out / drydock records, underwater diver inspection.
  - Class society / registration certificates, skipper/crew license validation, navigational warranty limits.
- **Adjustment & Quantum**:
  - Shipyard repair estimates, engine OEM parts invoices, salvage and towage services (Lloyd's Open Form LOF / towage contract).
  - New-for-old deductions where policy wording specifies aging deductions on sails, covers, and machinery.

| Yacht | Technical Specialist | — | Chartered Engineer and Average Adjuster | Same authorized professional |

## System interpretation

- Store workflow responsibilities independently from employee job titles.
- Allow several investigators or contributors on one report.
- Allow the same authorized person to hold two responsibilities, but require two explicit recorded assignments.
- Require the approver signature state and approval date before issue.
- Keep prior issued versions immutable.
- Do not grant final approval through the generic administrator role.

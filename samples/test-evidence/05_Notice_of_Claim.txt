NATURE AND EXTENT OF DAMAGE:

Upon taking delivery of Container CSLU 7234561 on 13 July 2026, our
receiving team observed that the container door seals appeared intact.
However, upon opening and destuffing on 14 July 2026 at our warehouse
(Beirut Port Free Zone, Warehouse 7), the following damage was discovered:

  1. WATER DAMAGE — Cases 3, 5, 7, and 9 (CNC Milling Machines JXP-500,
     S/N: JXP500-26-003, -005, -007, -009):
     Extensive moisture/water ingress inside the wooden crates.
     Visible rust and corrosion on unpainted machined surfaces, spindle
     housings, and ball screw assemblies. Water staining on Siemens 840D
     control units. Wooden crate interiors wet; silica gel desiccant packs
     saturated.

  2. PHYSICAL IMPACT DAMAGE — Case 11 (CNC Milling Machine JXP-500,
     S/N: JXP500-26-011):
     Crate shows evidence of being dropped or struck during handling.
     Machine base cracked; spindle misaligned; tool changer arm bent.
     Control panel display shattered.

  3. TILTING / IMPROPER HANDLING — Case 12 (CNC Milling Machine JXP-500,
     S/N: JXP500-26-012):
     Crate received tilted on its side. Machine shifted within crate.
     Levelling bolts sheared; coolant reservoir cracked and leaked.

  TOTAL UNITS AFFECTED: 6 of 12 milling machines (50% of consignment)

Container CSLU 7234562 (20'GP) was received without visible external
damage. Contents are being inspected but no damage has been discovered
to date.
